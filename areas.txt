Central Market is one of the most vibrant and well-known shopping hubs of Delhi, situated right in the heart of South Delhi, i.e., Lajpat Nagar, with its precise location being in the centre of Lajpat Nagar Part 2. The market is one of the most sought-after shopping destinations for residents of South Delhi, especially for those who value brands, class, and prefer a one-stop solution for all their shopping needs. 

It is located right next to the Lajpat Nagar Metro Station and is also well connected via Ring Road, which makes commuting extremely convenient. Whether it is shopping for jewellery, exploring the latest launches of your favourite brands, enjoying a movie night with friends, or planning a casual dine-out, this market serves as a go-to destination fulfilling all needs and desires with its lively and energetic atmosphere. It caters to visitors from every income group and houses retail outlets of almost every brand you can think of, from Apple to Tata and from Haldiram’s to Bata.
