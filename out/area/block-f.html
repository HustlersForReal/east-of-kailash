About Block F

Block F is a well-established residential pocket of East of Kailash, with a mix of independent homes, builder floors and low-rise residential properties in a relatively calm, established neighbourhood. It is particularly convenient for everyday needs, with banks, financial services, hotels, restaurants and local retail available nearby, while McDonald's and Spring Meadows Hospital are notable establishments in the locality. The block is situated close to Iskcon Chowk, Garhi Neem Chowk and Kailash Colony, with Kailash Colony Metro Station providing convenient access to the Delhi Metro network and Nehru Place serving as a major nearby commercial hub. The area also benefits from parks, schools, healthcare facilities and other essential services, while its proximity to neighbouring blocks such as E, D and C keeps it well integrated with the wider East of Kailash neighbourhood. With limited large-scale new development and a predominantly low-rise residential character, Block F offers an established South Delhi setting with good connectivity and strong access to everyday amenities.
