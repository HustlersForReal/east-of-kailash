About Block A

Block A is a well-established residential pocket of East of Kailash along Captain Gaur Marg, with a mix of independent houses, builder floors, DDA flats and CGHS apartments. It is a relatively quiet and green part of South Delhi, surrounded by Block B, Amritpuri, Sri Niwaspuri, Kailash Hills and Kailash Colony, while major areas such as Greater Kailash, Lajpat Nagar and Nehru Place are also easily accessible. The block is particularly well placed for everyday convenience, with nearby markets, cafés, schools, hospitals, banks and other local services, while the Lajpat Nagar and Greater Kailash M Block markets serve as major shopping and commercial destinations. The area is also close to some of East of Kailash's best-known landmarks, including the ISKCON Temple and Lotus Temple, with Nehru Place Market and Nehru Place Metro Station providing access to the wider commercial and transport network. Bluebells School International, Chandra Arya Vidya Mandir and other schools are located in the surrounding locality, while Kailash Colony adds further educational and healthcare options. Overall, Block A combines an established residential environment with strong road connectivity, nearby markets and several prominent South Delhi landmarks, making it a practical location for both homeowners and property seekers.
