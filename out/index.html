Our Heritage

Origin of East of Kailash

East of Kailash is a product of Delhi's great planned expansion. As the capital grew southward in the decades after independence, the Delhi Development Authority carved out organised residential enclaves across South Delhi — and this colony, laid out in neat Blocks A to G just east of the historic Kailash pocket, got the name it still carries with pride.

The plan was simple and far-sighted: wide roads, generous plot sizes, parks within walking distance of every home, and dedicated commercial centres to serve daily needs. Allottees moved into DDA flats and residential plots, and over time the colony matured into one of South Delhi's most balanced addresses — equally loved by families, professionals and investors.

As neighbouring Nehru Place grew into Asia's largest commercial hub and the Ring Road corridor came alive, East of Kailash found itself sitting at a remarkable junction — calm, green residential living on one side, and some of Delhi's biggest commercial and institutional landmarks on the other.

Today, with its two buzzing Community Centre markets, its own cinema hall, schools, hospitals and nursing homes inside the colony, East of Kailash stands complete — a self-sufficient pincode where everything you need lives within a few minutes of your doorstep.
